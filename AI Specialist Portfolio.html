How I work with AI

Talking to AI is a skill with a method

Step 1

Frame the task

State the goal, the audience and what a good result looks like before typing the first prompt.

Step 2

Give context

Share the facts, examples and limits the model cannot guess. Better input gives better output.

Step 3

Check the output

Verify claims, run the code, read for tone. A fluent answer is not the same as a correct one.

Step 4

Refine and decide

Adjust the prompt, repeat, and keep the final judgment with a person who owns the result.

Steps 3 and 4 loop until the result holds up.
